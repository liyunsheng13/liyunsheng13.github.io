Yunsheng Li is a Member of Technical Staff at Microsoft AI. His research interests include large language models (post-training, reinforcement learning, and agents for SWE and security) and multi-modal large language models. His representative works include MAI-Code-1, [Phi-4-mini](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-multimodal-instruct), [Phi-3-vision](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-3-vision-128k-instruct), [MicroNet](http://www.svcl.ucsd.edu/projects/micronet/) and [BDL](http://www.svcl.ucsd.edu/projects/bdl).

Projects
======
- **2025-present:** MAI-Code-1 and MAI-Cyber-1, the in-house models at Microsoft AI for SWE and cybersecurity tasks.
- **2024-2025:** [Phi-4-mini](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-multimodal-instruct) and [Phi-4-mini-reasoning](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-mini-reasoning), small language models with multi-modal and reasoning capabilities.
- **2023-2024:** [Phi-3-vision](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-3-vision-128k-instruct) and [Phi-3.5-vision](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-3.5-vision-instruct), small multi-modal language models.
- **2022-2023:** The background removal models used in [Windows Paint and Photos](https://blogs.windows.com/windows-insider/2023/09/07/background-removal-in-paint-begins-rolling-out-to-windows-insiders) and [Microsoft Designer](https://create.microsoft.com/en-us/features/image-background-remover).
- **2015-2021:** Ph.D. at University of California, San Diego, on [efficient neural network architecture design and domain adaptation](http://www.svcl.ucsd.edu/people/yunsheng/thesis.pdf).
